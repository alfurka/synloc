# Prospective diagnostic criteria

Declared **before fitting or evaluating the expanded SIPP model**. These are pragmatic study diagnostics, not privacy standards, population-inference guarantees, or a claim that other thresholds are universally appropriate.

For the first fixed configuration (50 requested clusters, minimum 100 people, ten synthetic draws), run the complete evaluation first. The mixture is a defensible candidate for a limited grid only if **all** these initial gates are met:

1. **Schema and validity:** direct and independent-loaded samples agree under identical seeds; all decoded records have valid categories and declared public bounds, and children do not exceed household size. Disclose all repair counts and check whether constraints apply to originals. No use of confidential min/max.
2. **Prevalence and support:** every original category constituting at least 1% of the sample, and the earnings/hours zero rates, have absolute mean synthetic error no greater than 5 percentage points. Report rarer groups separately even if this threshold does not assess them.
3. **Transitions:** for each employment, insurance and marital transition whose original starting category has at least 200 adjacent-quarter observations, the absolute synthetic mean conditional transition-probability error is at most 10 percentage points. An absent synthetic starting category fails, not zero error. Report the full transition matrices.
4. **Regression:** pooled and individual-FE specifications are identified in the original and every replicate. For every original coefficient with a finite nonzero person-clustered CR0 SE, compare the absolute difference between its mean synthetic coefficient and the original to twice that original SE. For each key FE coefficient (`hours`, `jobs`, `household_size`, `children`, `poverty_ratio`, and sex-by-hours interaction), the mean synthetic CR0 SE is between 0.75 and 1.25 times the original SE. These tolerances do not imply coverage or account for synthesis uncertainty.
5. **Disclosure:** required configured contributor-count checks pass and all other disclosure risks remain explicitly unassessed or flagged. This is not a clearance or formal privacy guarantee.

If any gate fails, **stop after the initial experiment**, publish all diagnostic results and document the failed gates. Do not tune decoder thresholds, filter out nonworkers, collapse rare categories, add another method, or run a broad parameter search to conceal poor results. The original and model-implied first/second moments are not independent validation; use transitions, subgroup distributions, support and SEs as substantive checks.
